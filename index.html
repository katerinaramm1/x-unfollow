Who doesn't follow me back on X?

A free tool. Upload your own X data archive and see who you follow that doesn't follow you back. Your files never leave your device.

1. Get your archive from X

  1. Open X → Settings and privacy → Your account → Download an archive of your data. If the link doesn't open, go there through the Settings menu.
  2. Confirm your identity and click Request archive.
  3. X prepares it and notifies you. This can take a day or more.
  4. Download the .zip file when it is ready.

The archive is a snapshot from the day you requested it, so results are "as of that day".

2. Upload it here

Drop your .zip here, or choose it.
Big archive, or the zip won't load? Unzip it and choose just follower.js and following.js from the data folder (select both together). They are tiny.

3. Your results

Mutuals 0 Not following you back 0 Fans you don't follow 0

Follow-back rate = the share of the accounts you follow that follow you back.

X's archive contains account IDs only, not usernames. Tap Open profile to see who it is on X, then unfollow there if you like. Tick the box once you've done it. This page never logs in to X or unfollows for you.

Privacy

Your archive is read and processed only in your browser. The files are not uploaded, saved or sent anywhere, and nothing is stored once you close or reload the page. The page does not ask for your X login or password.

The page counts visits with a simple, cookie-free visit counter that records page views only, never your files or results. Feedback is sent only if you choose to submit the feedback form, which is an outside service. Links to X and to the feedback form are outside links.
