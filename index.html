Who doesn't follow me back on X?

A free tool. Upload your own X data archive and see who you follow that doesn't follow you back. Your files never leave your device.

1. Get your archive from X

  1. Open X → Settings and privacy → Your account → Download an archive of your data. If the link doesn't open, go there through the Settings menu.
  2. Enter your X password when it asks, then click Request archive. (X may also ask for a verification code.)
  3. X prepares it and notifies you. This can take a day or more.
  4. Download the .zip file when it is ready.

The archive is a snapshot from the day you requested it, so results are "as of that day".

Don't forget to come back! Set a reminder for about 30 hours from now ():

2. Upload it here

Drop your .zip here, or choose it.
Big archive, or the zip won't load? Unzip it and choose just follower.js and following.js from the data folder (select both together). They are tiny.

Follow-back rate = the share of the accounts you follow that follow you back.

X's archive contains account IDs only, not usernames. Tap Open profile to go straight to that person's profile on X, then use the Following button there to unfollow. When you tap Open profile, the account is crossed out and a short pause starts, so you don't act too fast on X. Use Undo if you opened it by mistake, or Not following if you were no longer following that account. This page never logs in to X or unfollows for you.

Privacy

Your archive is read and processed only in your browser. The files are not uploaded, saved or sent anywhere, and nothing is stored once you close or reload the page. The page does not ask for your X login or password.

The page counts visits with a simple, cookie-free visit counter that records page views only, never your files or results. The total shown at the bottom of the page comes from that counter. Feedback is sent only if you choose to submit the feedback form, which is an outside service. Links to X and to the feedback form are outside links.

This tool is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp.
